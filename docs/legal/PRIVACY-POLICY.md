## 2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal data the Mizan Connect mobile app handles, why, who receives it, how long it is kept, and what rights you have. It applies to people who use the app as a client of an accounting or advisory firm that uses the Mizan platform ("your accountant").

It does not cover your accountant's own handling of your information outside the app. Your agreement with your accountant covers that.

## 3. Who decides how your data is used

Two parties are involved, in different roles.

- **Your accountant** decides what financial and business information is kept about your company and why, as part of the service they provide to you. For that information, your accountant is the controller and Mizan processes it on your accountant's instructions.
- **Mizan** decides how the app itself works. For your account details, your use of the app, security records and support requests, Mizan is the controller.

[TO CONFIRM by the lawyer: that this split matches the contracts between Mizan and the accounting firms, and between the firms and their clients.]

## 4. The data we handle

| Kind of data | Examples | Where it comes from |

| Account details | Email address, name, the company you belong to, your role | You; your accountant, who invites you |

## 6. The AI advisor

When you use the AI advisor, the text of your question, any file you attach and the figures needed to answer are sent to our AI provider, **Anthropic, PBC** (the Claude service), which processes them to produce an answer and returns it to the app.

- The app asks for your agreement before the first use. [TO CONFIRM: the consent screen is built before release; store readiness blocker 7.]
- Answers are advisory. Nothing the AI produces is entered into your books or sent to any authority unless a person approves it.
- [TO CONFIRM by the owner and lawyer: the provider's terms on retention and on not using this data to train models, and the country where it is processed.]

When you record a spoken question, the recording is sent to a speech-to-text provider to be turned into text (see section 7).

You can use the rest of the app without using the AI advisor.

## 10. Deleting your account

You can ask for your account to be deleted from inside the app (Settings, Delete my account) or by writing to privacy@mizan.team. [TO CONFIRM: the in-app option is built before release; store readiness blocker 1. A public web page for the same request is also required by Google Play.]

When an account is deleted, your sign-in details and personal profile are removed. Financial records that the law requires your company or your accountant to keep are not deleted before that period ends; they are kept only for that purpose.

## 11. Your rights

Subject to the law that applies to you, you may ask to: see the personal data held about you and receive a copy; have inaccurate data corrected; have data deleted; restrict or object to certain processing; withdraw a consent you gave, without affecting what was done before; and receive your data in a portable form.

Write to privacy@mizan.team. We answer within [TO CONFIRM: for example 30 days]. Where your accountant is the controller of the data concerned, we pass your request to them. You may also complain to the UAE Data Office or the competent authority. [TO CONFIRM by the lawyer.]

## 12. Security

- All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit.
- Your sign-in session is kept in the device's protected storage.
- Sign-in is by a one-time code sent to your email; there is no password to steal.
- Approving a change your accountant proposes requires a fresh one-time code.
- The service is built so that each client can reach only their own company's data, enforced on the server. [TO CONFIRM: by the server security review listed in the store readiness audit, before this sentence is published.]

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## 13. Children

The app is for business use by adults. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
